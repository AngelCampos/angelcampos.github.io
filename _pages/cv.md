Research Experience
======
* Postdoctoral researcher: Schraga Schwartz Lab
  * Development of Pan-Mod-seq, a high-throughput sequencing technology for simultane-
ous RNA modification sequencing of up to 16 different RNA modifications (*Cell*, 2025).
  * Refinement and release of txtools, an R package for transcriptome-wise RNA-seq data
processing and analysis (*Nucleic Acids Res.*, 2024).
* PhD research student: Schraga Schwartz Lab
  * Development of MAZTER-seq, an antibody-independent method for high-throughput
detection and quantitation of N6-methyladenosine (m6A). Allowing us to model m6A
installation levels in eukaryotic mRNA from yeast and mouse, which were then vali-
dated by orthogonal methods (*Cell*, 2019).
  * Initial development of txtools, an R package that processes RNA-seq reads into
transcriptomic-oriented data tables for efficient and simplified data analysis.
* MSc student: Enrique Hernandez Lemus Lab
  * Pathway Analysis: State of the art. A review article on gene-set enrichment analysis tools (*Frontiers in Physiology*, 2015).
* BSc student: Claudia Segal Kischinevzky
  * Characterization of an acatalasemic S. cerevisiae complemented with catalases from euryhaline D. hansenii
